About 247 Lifeline

Connecting Healthcare Professionals with Trusted Employers Across the United Kingdom.

Who We Are

247 Lifeline is a trusted healthcare recruitment agency dedicated to supplying skilled healthcare professionals to hospitals, care homes, nursing homes, supported living services and community healthcare providers throughout the United Kingdom.

We specialise in recruiting compassionate Healthcare Assistants, Support Workers, Registered Nurses and other healthcare professionals, ensuring every placement meets the highest standards of quality, compliance and patient care.

Our experienced recruitment team works closely with both clients and candidates, building long-term relationships through honesty, professionalism and exceptional service.
